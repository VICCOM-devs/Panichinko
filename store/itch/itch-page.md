**Details → Description** (paste as is; the itch editor accepts this formatting)

> **One player drops the coins. Four try not to get squished.**
>
> Panichinko is an asymmetric party game set inside a giant pachinko machine. The **Biggie** works the machine from outside, aiming coins, tilting the board and hunting for multikills. Up to four **Tinies** run, jump, dash and grapple between the pegs, trying to stay alive and rack up points. Every round lasts two minutes and is loud, bloody and stupid in the best way.
>
> ⚠️ Cartoon blood and dismemberment. Prefer it clean? Switch Death style to **Confetti** in Settings.
>
> **What's in the demo**
> - **Online play, 1 vs 4.** Make a room and share the 4-letter code, or hit Quick match. Peer-to-peer for up to 5 players, and bots fill the empty seats.
> - **Two very different roles.** The Biggie scores with slots, spinner hits, tulips and squished Tinies. Tinies score by surviving high up on the board, keeping a streak, surfing on falling coins, doing flips and grabbing shinies.
> - **5 boards:** Classic, Clean White, Neon Shift (moving pegs), Haunted (vanishing pegs) and Tunnels (coins shoot through the walls).
> - **Twitch streamer mode.** Your chat votes on powerups and events mid-round by typing 1, 2 or 3. It only reads chat and never logs in or posts.
> - Offline play against bots (easy, normal, hard), with the Biggie bot up to "brutal".
> - Ragdolls, procedural music and four Tiny voices that scream a lot.
>
> **Controls**
>
> *Biggie:* move the mouse to aim, click to drop a coin, right-click to tilt. Or use A/D to aim, Space to drop, Z/X to tilt and Q for your power.
>
> *Tiny:* A/D to move, Space to jump, Shift to dash, E or click to grapple (aims at the mouse), W/S to reel the rope in and out, Q for your power.
>
> **Early demo.** This is a work-in-progress build made to test the feel and the rules. Expect rough edges, and tell us what breaks in the comments. Panichinko is heading to Steam later.
>
